# Changelog

## 1.0.0

- Initial public portable camouflage construction release.
- Eight vanilla camouflage designs in US and Soviet styles, with carryable bundles and reciprocal material recovery.
- Optional native shovel-building animation for an equipped shovel; tool-free building remains available.
- Production packaging excludes the private proving ground, test fixtures, logs, profiles, diagnostic commands and local launchers.

Validation details and remaining limits are recorded in `docs/VALIDATION.md`.

# Portable Camo Nets

Carry, deploy, and recover camouflage hides in Arma Reforger. Available on the Workshop; this repo contains the **1.0.0 source**.

Carry bundles on your back or in vehicle inventory, drop one, and deploy a hide. Matching bundles can be in your inventory or within five metres. Pack the hide to recover them. No construction truck is required.

## Equipment

US LCSS and Soviet MKT styles are separate. Each bundle weighs **12 kg**, occupies **20 L**, and is configured to cost **80 supplies**, with no rank lock. Small, medium, and large hides use **1, 2, and 3 bundles**.

[Equipment and usage](docs/USAGE.md) lists the eight designs, build times, and compatibility limits.

## Install

Enable Portable Camo Nets in Arma Reforger and add it to your server's mod list. Only the vanilla game is required. Bundles appear in US and Soviet equipment catalogs; mods replacing those catalogs may need compatibility work.
